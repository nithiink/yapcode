Conversation panel — latest-first options

Goal: by default show only the most recent messages with no prominent scrollbar, and give a control at the top to review older messages on demand.

Each card is a working mini-demo using the real theme. Click the toolbars to feel the behavior. The sample thread has 9 items (turns + a tool call) so there's history to reveal. Pick the one you want and I'll wire it into the real Conversation panel.

A. Segmented “Latest / All” toggle
A two-way switch in the panel header. Latest shows the last few messages, auto-height, no scrollbar. All opens the full capped, scrollable history with the slim bar.
Best when: you want a single obvious switch. Trade-off: binary — no in-between sizes.

B. “Earlier messages” disclosure
Default shows only the latest messages, no scrollbar. A dashed row at the top — “↑ N earlier messages” — expands the full scrollable history inline. Collapses back with “Show latest”.
Best when: you want history truly hidden until asked. Trade-off: one extra click to start reviewing.

C. Scrollbar toggle + fade
A constant compact window always shows the latest, with a soft fade at the top hinting there’s more. You can already scroll (wheel/trackpad) with no visible bar; a “Scrollbar” toggle reveals the slim bar on demand. “Jump to latest” snaps back down.
Best when: you want the closest thing to today, just calmer. Trade-off: the bar is the only “options”, less explicit than a mode switch.

D. “Show” count selector
A small selector at the top picks how much history is visible: 3 · 8 · All. Low counts auto-size with no scrollbar; All caps the height and turns on the slim bar. Power users choose their density.
Best when: different users want different amounts. Trade-off: three choices is slightly more UI than a binary switch.
