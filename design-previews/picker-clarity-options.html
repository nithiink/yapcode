Two problems: (1) marking Gemini + CLI as recommended without shouting, (2) telling a new user what Gemini/OpenAI and CLI/SDK actually are.

Everything below is live — click segments, hover buttons, open the ⓘ popovers. Mix & match: pick one marker + one explainer (or a combo from section 3).

1 · Marking “recommended”

A — Corner dot

A 4px green dot tucked in the pill’s corner. Quietest possible signal; meaning arrives via tooltip on hover.

B — Caption under the row

Buttons stay untouched; one quiet line beneath the controls names the recommended pairing in plain words.

C — Check prefix

A small green ✓ baked into the recommended label. Legible without a legend, adds no width chrome.

D — Tinted segment

The recommended pill carries a faint green wash + hairline — felt more than read. Strongest when paired with a tooltip.

Like the current badge but a single short word, no border, 7.5px. About a third the footprint of “RECOMMENDED”.

Two choices before you talk

Voice is who you speak with — Gemini (cheapest, most reliable) or OpenAI (premium voices, native or via Azure). Engine is who does the work — CLI (the real Claude Code terminal on your Max plan) or SDK (headless, per-token billing). Gemini + CLI is the recommended pairing.
