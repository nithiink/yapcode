Claude Sessions — redesign options

Six directions. The point isn't only to declutter — it's that the controls should tell the user what they can do. Most people won't guess that they can watch the live CLI inside the browser, or carry the same session into their own terminal. Every option below uses the renamed, value-led labels in the glossary, shows the mode once, uses a calm status dot, abbreviates the home dir to ~, and drops the always-$0.0000 subscription cost.

Naming — what it is, why it's there, what to call it
